Is news trading allowed?

Yes, on the 1-Step and 2-Step challenges.

Is a stop loss required?

On the 1-Step funded account a stop loss is mandatory; trading without one is a soft breach. Your account must always stay inside the daily and overall drawdown limits.

Is there a consistency rule?

The 1-Step funded account has a 30% consistency rule that cannot be removed. The Limitless funded account has a 20% rule that can be removed with an add-on.

When can I request a payout?

1-Step and 2-Step: every Friday (1-Step minimum payout is 1% of the account). Limitless: every 14 days, or weekly with an add-on.

What are add-ons?

Add-ons remove or relax selected rules: the 2% max risk per trade and the 2x lot cap after a loss on 1-Step, and weekly payouts, the 20% consistency rule and the 2x martingale cap on Limitless. Weekend holding on Limitless cannot be changed by an add-on.

What happens if I breach my challenge?

On the 1-Step challenge you get 10% off your next challenge.

Which actions are prohibited?

Account sharing, copy trading from other users' accounts, latency arbitrage and any exploitation of platform errors.
