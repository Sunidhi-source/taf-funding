Is news trading allowed?

Yes. You can trade through high-impact news on challenge and funded accounts.

Is a stop-loss required?

No, but your account must stay inside the daily loss and drawdown limits at all times.

Is there a consistency rule?

No. Profits are judged on totals, not on how evenly they were made.

How fast are payouts?

Payout requests are processed within 24 hours.

Which actions are prohibited?

Account sharing, copy trading from other users' accounts, latency arbitrage and any exploitation of platform errors.
